Payloads

At any given time in the program, a number of payloads are in various stages of preparation for future flights. The payloads we fly range from sentimental items flown to near-space or simple sensor-in-a-box type payloads to complex locomotive payloads with several moving parts, payloads with radio transceivers powerful enough to stream data from the balloon in-flight, and everything in between.
